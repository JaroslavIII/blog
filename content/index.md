I'm Jaroslav Filinger, a student of Economics and Finance at the Institute of Economic Studies (IES), Faculty of Social Sciences, Charles University in Prague.

My interests lie where economics meets history: economic history, political economy, and the ideas that shape the policies nations choose. I am building the quantitative skills to study these questions rigorously, and I plan to continue with graduate study in economics.

This site brings together my writing, achievements and projects, from essays and book notes to interactive data analyses. More [about me](about.md).
